## Question

We sell to customers in the EU. What do we need to know about personal data in NetLicensing?

## Answer

NetLicensing is run by Labs64 GmbH in Munich, Germany. When you store your customers' personal data in NetLicensing,
Labs64 processes it on your behalf, and you can sign a [Data Processing Agreement (DPA)](data-processing-agreement-dpa)
with us for that.

The [Privacy Center](privacy-center) explains what data is processed and how to handle requests from your customers.
You can [look up and correct](how-to-access-and-update-licensee-data), [export](how-to-export-licensee-data) and
[delete](how-to-delete-licensee-data) a licensee's data, and
[check the consent](how-to-access-and-verify-consent-given-by-end-customer) they gave in the Shop.

The less personal data you store, the less you have to worry about. Where you can, use a pseudonymous licensee number,
such as an installation ID, rather than an e-mail address.
