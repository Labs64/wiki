## Question

What is NetLicensing and what is it used for?

## Answer

Labs64 NetLicensing is a cloud service for managing software licenses, sometimes called Licensing-as-a-Service (LaaS).
Software vendors set up how their products are licensed, using built-in [licensing models](licensing-models) such as
trials, subscriptions, SaaS plans, feature licensing, floating, node-locked, pay-per-use and quota. Their applications
then ask the [REST API](restful-api) what a customer may use.

Customers buy, renew and upgrade in the white-label [NetLicensing Shop](netlicensing-shop) or in the vendor's own
online shop. Vendors manage everything in the Management Console, through the API, or from AI agents with the
[MCP server](netlicensing-mcp). Nobody has to build or run a license server.

NetLicensing is made by Labs64 GmbH in Munich, Germany. The [NetLicensing Overview](overview) and
[Getting Started](getting-started) are good next stops.
